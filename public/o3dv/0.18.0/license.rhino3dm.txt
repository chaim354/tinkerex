rhino3dm 8.17.0 (MIT) — rhino3dm.min.js and rhino3dm.wasm in this directory, copied unmodified from
the npm package by bin/build-o3dv (https://registry.npmjs.org/rhino3dm/-/rhino3dm-8.17.0.tgz).

The npm package ships no licence file; its package.json says "license": "MIT" and "author":
"Robert McNeel & Associates and contributors". The texts below come from the upstream repository
instead: github.com/mcneel/rhino3dm at tag 8.17.0, commit
1e0c80e6b9cf7ff3c1579d287e178b21634a2a6d. npm records no gitHead for this version, so what ties
that tag to the published bytes is the version number, not a recorded commit.

rhino3dm.wasm is more than rhino3dm's own code. At that tag, src/CMakeLists.txt builds the
WebAssembly target (its EMSCRIPTEN branch: `add_executable(rhino3dm ...)`) from rhino3dm's
bindings plus, statically, openNURBS (the src/lib/opennurbs submodule), the zlib and android_uuid
sources that live inside openNURBS, and Draco (the src/lib/draco submodule, McNeel's fork). Each
of their notices follows. openNURBS also carries a freetype263/ directory; that target does not
compile it — its source globs are the top level of opennurbs/, opennurbs/zlib/ and
opennurbs/android_uuid/ only.

Not covered here, for this file or the other two .wasm builds in this directory: the Emscripten
runtime and the C/C++ standard libraries the toolchain links into every Emscripten build. Those
were not audited for this notice.
